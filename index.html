The board

Players and teams we can put a number on. How these are chosen

TONIGHT’S PICKS
Every pick is logged here at the line and price shown, before tip, and settled against the official box score tomorrow morning. Nothing is added after the fact and nothing is removed when it loses — the log has all of them, winners and losers, with how close each one came.
THE RECORD
Every pick we publish is logged with the line and price it was published at, and settled against the official NBA box score the next morning. The log is never edited after the fact — grading only adds a result to a row that already exists. Staking is flat, one unit a pick, so a good run and a lucky one cannot be made to look different.

We publish the win rate at 30 graded picks. Not before, and not later. Below that number a 3–0 start and an 0–3 start carry the same information, which is none, and showing either would be dishonest. The count and the full archive are up from pick one: see every pick in the log, or download the CSV.
PRA WATCH  ·  PLAYING TONIGHT
OUR PRA is the player’s typical points+rebounds+assists across three seasons — roughly where a book would set him, and a measure of how forecastable his minutes are, not a prediction for tonight. LOCKED means his minutes have held steady across those seasons; MOVED means they haven’t, and the PRA figure is correspondingly softer.

BOOK PRA is the combined points+rebounds+assists line the sportsbooks are quoting tonight, via The Odds API. We take the median line across books rather than the average, so one stale number can’t drag it, and we strip the vig from each book’s pair of prices before averaging them into P(OVER). The book count beside the label says how many were quoting.

VS OURS is that market line minus our figure. It is not an edge. The market price already contains tonight’s injuries, rest and matchup, none of which a three-season average knows about, so a gap is a question worth logging rather than a bet. PTS, REB and AST break the same player into his three legs. A column headed LINE is the book’s number alone; one headed LINE → PROJ also carries a Covers.com projection for that leg, which exists only for the handful of props their model chooses to flag. The heading changes rather than the two being blended, because a traded line and a model’s output are different claims.

Where no book quotes a combined line, the PRA column falls back to the three Covers projections added together and is labelled SUMMED. Book lines are never added into that sum — doing so would quietly turn a market price into a model output.
CORROBORATED PROPS  ·  TONIGHT
Every figure here is Covers.com’s, captured daily. Not our numbers and not our recommendations. Covers runs two desks that work independently — named writers picking sides, and a projection model — and a prop shows here only when both landed on the same side of the same line.

LINE is the number quoted, PROJ what their model returns, EDGE the gap between them, EV their stated expected value. Ordered by EV. A projection sitting within 0.5 of the line is dropped however large its stated EV, and a player two writers quoted on opposite sides is dropped entirely. Picks we read but did not show are counted above and kept in the log, since whether the unbacked ones land is itself worth knowing.
