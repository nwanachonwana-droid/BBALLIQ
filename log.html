The log

Every pick we have published, with the line it was published at, the number it finished on, and how far apart those two were. Winners and losers both. Nothing is removed and nothing is edited after the fact.

MARGIN is the final number minus the line, signed in the direction of the pick: positive means it cleared, negative means it missed, and a small negative is a loss that was one possession from a win. Nothing about a near miss makes it a win, but a card full of them is a different problem from a card that was never close.

UNITS are at the published American price, one unit staked flat on every pick. Settled from the official box score the morning after. Download the whole archive as CSV.
