The log

Every pick we have published, with the line it was published at, the number it finished on, and how far apart those two were. Winners and losers both. Nothing is removed and nothing is edited after the fact.

Outside reads, graded

Not our picks and not bets. Covers.com runs two desks that work independently — named writers picking sides, and a projection model — and we grade every pick from both, every night, whether or not we showed it. Twenty a night answers “does agreement between two expert reads predict anything” far sooner than our own three a night ever could.

CORROBORATED means a writer took a side and the projection agreed. CONFLICT means they took opposite sides of the same number, so exactly one of them is wrong — that row is the fastest read on which surface to believe, and the only one that can settle it. UNBACKED is a writer pick with no projection to check it against.

Graded ungated: the filters decide what goes on the board, not what gets studied. A pick we declined to show still says whether the surface that made it was right, and dropping those would bias the sample toward the ones we already liked.

MARGIN is the final number minus the line, signed in the direction of the pick: positive means it cleared, negative means it missed, and a small negative is a loss that was one possession from a win. Nothing about a near miss makes it a win, but a card full of them is a different problem from a card that was never close.

UNITS are at the published American price, one unit staked flat on every pick. Settled from the official box score the morning after. Download the whole archive as CSV.
