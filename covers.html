Covers.com

Someone else’s props, captured every night and graded against the box score. Not our card.

READ THIS FIRST
These are not our picks and we do not bet them. They are published by Covers.com, and we capture them because a free public list is a useful benchmark — if it beat the closing line we would want to know, and if it does not, that is worth knowing too.

Betting a whole list of props, from anyone, is how people lose money quickly. Fifteen correlated overs at −115 need an improbable night to come out ahead, and no list is sized for your bankroll because whoever wrote it has never seen it. Our own card is short on purpose and lives on the board.
CORROBORATED PROPS  ·  TONIGHT
Every figure here is Covers.com’s, captured daily. Covers runs two desks that work independently — named writers picking sides, and a projection model — and a prop shows here only when both landed on the same side of the same line.

LINE is the number quoted, PROJ what their model returns, EDGE the gap between them, EV their stated expected value. Ordered by EV. A projection sitting within 0.5 of the line is dropped however large its stated EV, and a player two writers quoted on opposite sides is dropped entirely. The ones we read but did not show are still counted and graded below.
HOW THE LIST HAS DONE
Every Covers prop is logged the night it is published and settled the next morning, including the ones that never appear in the table above. We hold the hit rates back until 100 settled picks — the same discipline we apply to our own record, for the same reason: below that a hot start and a cold one carry identical information. The count and the full archive are up from pick one.
